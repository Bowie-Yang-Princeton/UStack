# The four device structures in UStack

Research notes behind the built-in templates (`ustack/data/templates/*.json`, generated by
`tools/make_builtin_templates.py`). Read from the arXiv versions of the papers' Methods and from the
lab's own documents (*Design – Autocad DRC.docx*, *Fab Manual* by Sihong, *tTMD_device_geometry.pptx*,
*WSe2 device structure.pptx*) on 2026-11-16.

**How the numbers are used.** A number a paper states is stored as a *suggestion* on the template
field (`suggested`, `suggested_source`), never as a value. The user accepts it ("Use") or enters
their own; anything left empty is asked for. A paper's material that is not in
`config/materials.csv` is left empty with the paper's choice in the layer's `literature` note —
UStack never swaps in a lab metal by itself.

Layers below are listed **bottom → top**, as in the specification.

---

## 1. TMD · Xiaodong Xu (University of Washington) — `xu_tmd`

Sources: H. Park et al., arXiv:2406.09591 (2024) Methods + ED Fig. 2; H. Park et al., Nature 622, 74
(2023), arXiv:2308.02657 Methods; the lab's tTMD deck (slides 10–11, 24); the lab's WSe2 structure
slide; the user's rules in the request.

| # | Layer | Function | Literature |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 285 nm SiO2 / Si | substrate | "p-doped Si/SiO2" for exfoliation |
| 2 | Graphite | bottom gate | few-layer; thickness not stated |
| 3 | Bottom hBN | bottom-gate dielectric | thickness not stated |
| 4 | Pt Hall-bar contacts (lab: Ti/Pt) | Hall-bar contacts | **Pt 8 nm**, e-beam evaporated on the BG stack, AFM-cleaned, RMS < 300 pm |
| 5–6 | TMD monolayer, two halves | device layer | AFM-tip cut; MoTe2 in Ar glovebox (O2/H2O < 0.1 ppm) |
| 7 | Middle hBN (thin) | contact-gate dielectric | "a thin BN as the contact gate dielectric" |
| 8 | Pt contact gates (lab: Ti/Pt) | contact gate | **Pt 8 nm** |
| 9 | Cr/Au leads | leads | **Cr/Au 5/70 nm** |
| 10 | hBN | top-gate dielectric | — |
| 11 | Graphite | top gate | — |
| 12 | Top-most hBN | encapsulation | — |

Process: (1) PC picks up bottom hBN + graphite BG, release on Si; (2) EBL + Pt Hall-bar contacts on
the BG stack, contact-mode AFM clean **[Stage 2: pre-pattern contacts]**; (3) cut the TMD, pick up
thin hBN, half 1, rotate, half 2, release on the BG stack; (4) wash, AFM clean, EBL Pt contact gates
+ Cr/Au leads, AFM clean **[Stage 2: contact gates + contacts]**; (5) transfer hBN/graphite/hBN top
gate.

Lab rules (from the request, *approved* in `rules.json`): larger bottom hBN
(`xu.bottom_hbn_larger`), thin middle hBN
(`xu.middle_hbn_max_thickness_nm` — **limit not given, asked for**), middle-hBN edge ≥ 1 µm from the
WSe2 edges (`xu.middle_hbn_edge_margin_um`).

> Interpretation to confirm: "middle BN edge at least 1 µm away from the WSe2 edges to allow contact
> gates" is implemented as *the middle hBN encloses the TMD with ≥ 1 µm to spare on every side*, so a
> contact gate on the middle hBN can never touch the TMD. Stage 2 also checks that no contact gate
> touches the TMD outside the middle hBN.

Metal note: the papers use Pt alone (no adhesion layer); the lab list has Ti/Pt, so the template uses
Ti/Pt with the Ti thickness left to the user (0 allowed).

## 2. TMD · Kin Fai Mak / Jie Shan, Yiyu Xia (Cornell) — `mak_tmd`

Sources: Y. Xia et al., Nature 637, 833 (2025), arXiv:2405.14784 Methods + ED Fig. 1; Y. Xia et al.,
arXiv:2508.02662 (2025) Methods + ED Fig. 1; K. Kang et al., arXiv:2501.02525 (in the lab deck);
the lab's tTMD deck (slides 3–6).

| # | Layer | Function | Literature |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 285 nm SiO2 / Si | substrate | |
| 2 | Ti/Pt electrodes, two columns | Hall-bar contacts (pre-patterned on the chip) | **Ti/Pt 5/25 nm** |
| 3 | Graphite, in the gap between the columns | bottom gate | few-layer |
| 4 | Bottom hBN (covers the BG, narrower than the WSe2) | bottom-gate dielectric | **12.0 nm** (2508.02662) |
| 5–6 | WSe2 monolayer, two halves | device layer | AFM-tip cut |
| 7 | Top hBN | top-gate dielectric | **3.4 nm** (2508.02662) |
| 8 | Graphite (smaller than BG) | top gate | TG ∩ BG overlap = channel |
| 9 | hBN cap | encapsulation | not stated |
| 10 | Contact gates | contact gate | **Ti/Pd 5/35 nm** (5/40 in 2405.14784) — *not in the lab list* |
| 11 | Split gates | split gates | Ti/Pd, same step — *not in the lab list* |

Electrode layout (stated by the lab, 2026-11-16): the Ti/Pt is pre-patterned on the chip as two
columns of parallel fingers with one long thin gap between the columns. The stack is released with
the graphite BG in the gap, never touching the Pt. The WSe2 is wider than the bottom hBN, so it
extends past the hBN edge onto the finger tips of both columns while staying gated in between.

Process: (1) EBL Ti/Pt electrodes (two columns) on the chip **[Stage 2: pre-pattern contacts]**;
(2) PC/PDMS picks up hBN cap, TG graphite, thin hBN, half 1, half 2 (twisted), bottom hBN, BG
graphite; (3) release at 200 °C (180 °C for MoTe2, Kang 2025) with the BG in the gap; (4) EBL
contact gates + split gates **[Stage 2: contact gates + split gates]**.

In UStack: Hall bar › Source / drain *Bent to the sides* (the end fingers of the two columns are
source and drain), W = the gap between the columns. Validation checks that the BG fits the gap
with spacing (`gate_in_gap`) and that the WSe2 is wider than every layer between it and the
electrodes (`contact_not_touching`); Stage 2 checks that the electrodes never touch the traced BG.

Published geometry: L ≈ 1.6 µm (centre-to-centre) → Hall-bar suggestion; the published channel
width (W ≈ 1.3 µm, 2508.02662) is the TG ∩ BG channel, not the electrode gap.
Rule `mak.tg_smaller_than_bg` (*literature*): TG smaller than BG; contact gates dope the TMD between
channel and Pt; split gates deplete BG-only regions.

## 3. Graphene · Liqiao Xia (MIT; the lab's DRC notes) — `xia_graphene`

Sources: L.-Q. Xia et al., arXiv:2310.12204 (helical trilayer graphene), Methods A; the lab's
*Design – Autocad DRC.docx* (section "Liqiao") and *Fab Manual* (Graphene Devices – New / Nov 2025).

| # | Layer | Function | Literature / lab |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 285 nm SiO2 / Si | substrate | paper: 285 nm SiO2/Si |
| 2 | Graphite | bottom gate | "few-layer graphene strip"; BG stack annealed 350 °C 12 h in vacuum, AFM-cleaned |
| 3 | Bottom hBN | bottom-gate dielectric | DRC: larger than the BG on 3 sides, BG sticks out on the 4th |
| 4 | Graphene | device layer | monolayer for the Hall-bar example; helical: laser-cut into 3, 1.8° per step, RT pick-up |
| 5 | Top hBN | top-gate dielectric | DRC: covers the good graphene |
| 6 | Metal top gate | top gate | paper: 25–65 nm Au on 2–5 nm Cr/Ti; Fab Manual: **Ti/Au 5/30 nm** (pending) |
| 7 | 1D edge contacts | Hall-bar contacts | paper: 63–75 nm Au on 2–5 nm Cr; Fab Manual: **Cr/Au 5/~60 nm** (pending) |

Process: bottom stack by PC → anneal → AFM clean; top stack (top hBN + graphene) by PC at RT;
release at 150–170 °C; bubble-free region by AFM; then **board etch → metal TG → Hall-bar (device)
etch → 1D contacts** (Stage 2 purposes of the same names). Etch recipes and rates are in
`rules.json` (`etch.*`, pending).

DRC rules copied into `rules.json` (all *pending*): contact width > 0.5 µm; 0.4 µm between contacts;
0.3 µm between graphene contact arms; corner radius > 0.05 µm; contacts ≥ 0.7 µm from graphite
BG/TG; Hall-bar arms ≥ ~1.5 µm beyond the BG for 1D contacts; metal TG > 0.4 µm; graphene∩BG needs
bottom BN, graphene∩TG needs top BN; TG extends beyond graphene; 1D contacts outside the BG; contact
metal thicker than BN/Gr/BN; etch-check windows in the board etch. Not yet encoded (need geometry the
v1 does not have): alignment-mark clearances ("leave clearance for 2 of 12"), local capacitance,
stitching patches, the scanning-probe considerations.

## 4. Rhombohedral graphene · Long Ju (MIT) — `ju_rhombohedral`

Sources: Z. Lu et al., Nature 626, 759 (2024), arXiv:2309.17436 (main text; no Methods in v1);
T. Han et al., arXiv:2305.03151 Methods; T. Han et al., arXiv:2408.15233 Methods.

| # | Layer | Function | Literature |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 285 nm SiO2 / Si | substrate | |
| 2 | Graphite | bottom gate | bottom stack prepared first |
| 3 | Bottom hBN | bottom-gate dielectric | straight edges misaligned to avoid a moiré (Han 2024) |
| 4 | Rhombohedral N-layer graphene | device layer | pentalayer (Lu 2024, Han 2024); domains by IR camera / near-field IR / Raman; cut by AFM (Han 2024) or fs laser (Han 2025) |
| 5 | Top hBN | top-gate dielectric | aligned only for a moiré device: ~0.77°, period ~11.5 nm (Lu 2024) |
| 6 | Graphite | top gate | Han 2025 stack: top hBN / graphite / middle hBN / …; Han 2024 used a NiCr top layer |
| 7 | hBN cap | encapsulation | |
| 8 | Cr/Au contacts | Hall-bar contacts | "Cr–Au for electrical connections"; thickness not stated |

Process: PPC film picks up the top stack (cap, TG, top hBN, graphene) and lands it on the prepared
bottom stack; EBL + RIE into a Hall bar; Cr/Au contacts.

## Things the papers do not state (asked for in the app)

hBN and graphite thicknesses for most layers; Hall-bar dimensions (except Mak 2025); metal
thicknesses for Long Ju's contacts; release temperatures for Xu; channel/contact geometry beyond the
images.
